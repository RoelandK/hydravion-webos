# Hydravion User Guide

Hey! Welcome to Hydravion. This is a little app that lets you watch your Floatplane subscriptions right on your LG TV. Its not official, just a fan made thing, but it works pretty well.

This guide walks you through everything you can do in the app. Grab the remote and lets go.

## Getting started and logging in

![Login screen](/docs/img/00-login.png)

The first time you open the app you land on the login screen. Its split in two.

On the left is the welcome panel. If you dont have a Floatplane account yet, there is a QR code that takes you to floatplane.com so you can make one. Its free and takes a minute.

On the right is where you sign in. If you are already a member, scan the QR code with your phone camera and it opens the Floatplane login page with a code already filled in. Confirm it on the phone and the TV signs in by itself, no typing on the TV needed.

If scanning is being fiddly, the code is also shown as text under the QR. You can type that into the website manually instead.

Once the TV confirms the login it loads your subscriptions and drops you on Home. From there on its smooth sailing.

## The sidebar

![The sidebar](/docs/img/02-sidebar.png)

The menu on the left hand side is your main way to get around. Press **Left** on the remote when you are at the left edge of a page and the sidebar opens up. Its a bit like the menu on YouTube TV.

From the top down you have:

- **Home** - your subscriptions feed
- **Watch History** - stuff you watched before
- **Watch Later** - your own personal list of saved videos
- **Activity** - comments you have made
- **Account** - your profile and billing
- **Discover** - find new creators
- **Live Streams** - jump straight to whoever is live right now
- **Search** - find videos
- **Settings** - tweak how the app behaves

Below that you see your subscriptions listed. If someone is live you get a little red LIVE badge next to there name. Pretty handy.

To close the sidebar, press **Right** or **Back**. You can also press **Left** again if you are on the first menu item.

## Home and browsing

![Home](/docs/img/01-home.png)

Home is where the magic happens. Its your subscriptions feed, layed out as a bunch of horizontal rows. Each row is one creator you follow. The newest videos are on the left, scroll right to see more.

At the very top you have a **Continue Watching** row. These are videos you started but didnt finish. The red bar under the thumbnail shows how far you got. There is a **Clear all** button if you want to reset all your progress.

Use **Up** and **Down** to move between rows, **Left** and **Right** to move within a row. Press **Enter** on a video to open it up.

Each video card shows the title, the release date and how many likes and dislikes it has. If you already liked or disliked a video you will see a little tag on the card saying so.

---

## The creator page

![Creator page](/docs/img/04-creator.png)

Click a creator header (the name above a row) to open there page. This is where you get the full overview.

You see:

- A big cover banner at the top
- The creator icon and name
- **Channel pills** - if the creator has subchannels (like Channel Super Fun or Techquickie) you can filter by them. Select a pill to only see videos from that channel.
- A search box to search within that creator
- A full grid of all their videos

Press **Back** to return to Home.

## Watching a video

![Details](/docs/img/06-details.png)

When you open a video you get the details page. This shows the big hero image, the title, description, when it was released and the channel it belongs to.

The buttons here are:

- **Play** - starts playback. If there are multiple quality options it asks which one you want first.
- **Watch later** - saves the video to your Watch Later list. The button changes to show a checkmark when its saved.
- **Back** - go back where you came from.
- **Clear resume** - if you have a resume position saved you get a button to clear it, so next time you can start from the beginning.

Underneath you can like or dislike the video. The counters show how many people liked and disliked it. Your choice is highlighted in blue.

Further down you have the **comments**. They show the author, their supporter badges, the date and like/dislike counts. Right now commenting is turned off in the app, but you can still read everything and like or dislike individual comments. If a comment is yours you can delete it with the X.

## The player

![Player](/docs/img/07-player.png)

The player is a fullscreen video. Moving the mouse or pressing any button brings up the controls, they hide themselves after a few seconds so you can watch without distractions.

The control bar at the bottom has:

- **Prev / Next** - jump to the previous or next video in the row you came from
- **Play / Pause** - obvious one
- **Progress bar** - shows where you are. Click or tap to seek.
- **Like / Dislike** - react to the video from the player
- **CC** - turn subtitles on and off, pick the track
- **Speed** - playback speed (0.5x up to 2x)
- **Mute** - mute the sound
- **HD** - quality picker (Auto or a specific resolution)
- **Info** - a geek panel with technical details like resolution, codec, bitrate and dropped frames. Good for nerds.

When the video ends it automatically plays the next one in the queue. After a few videos in a row it asks if you are still watching. Press **Yes, continue** to keep going or **Stop** to go back.

Your progress is saved as you watch, so if you leave and come back you can resume where you left off.

## Watch history

![Watch later](/docs/img/03-watchlater.png)

Watch History is the list of stuff you have watched, straight from Floatplane. Its not stored on your TV, it comes from the site itself, so it stays in sync with the website and your other devices.

Each card shows a green **Watched today** or **Watched yesterday** label and the date. There is a progress bar on the thumbnail showing how much you watched.

---

## Watch later

Watch Later is your own personal list. The website does not have this feature, so its app only and lives on your TV. Your saved videos are stored locally on the device.

To save a video, open it and press **Watch later**. The button turns into a checkmark to confirm.

To watch your list, open Watch Later from the sidebar. You can remove a single video with the X on the card, or press **Clear all** in the header to wipe the whole list.

If your list is empty you get a friendly message and a few suggestions from your watch history, in case you want to re-watch something. Handy.

---

## Activity

Activity shows your own comment history, pulled from Floatplane. Its a simple list of comments you have made, with the video title and the creator. Click one to jump to that video.

---

## Account

![Account](/docs/img/10-account.png)

The Account section is a small menu with:

- **Profile** - your username, email and such
- **Notifications** - turn notification channels on or off per creator
- **Connected accounts** - linked accounts like Discord
- **Security** - account security info
- **Billing - Subscriptions** - your active subscriptions
- **Billing - Invoices** - payment history, with dates and amounts

Press **Back** on a page to return to the menu, press **Back** again to leave.

---

## Discover

![Discover](/docs/img/09-discover.png)

Discover is the way to find new creators. The website is a bit hidden about this but the app shows you a full list.

You can:

- **Search** for a creator by name
- **Filter by category** using the pills at the top (All, Art, Gaming, Informational, Music, Technology and so on)

Each card shows the creator icon, name, category and a short description. Open a card to see their channels and subscription plans. If you want to subscribe, the app points you to floatplane.com to do it, since paying goes through the official site.

---

## Live streams

The Live Streams menu item jumps straight to whoever is live at the moment. If nobody is live you get a little toast saying so.

If there is a stream you get the live player with chat on the side. The chat shows messages as they come in. You can switch the layout between chat on the bottom or chat on the side with the layout button.

---

## Search

Search is server side, so it searches across all your subscriptions on Floatplane. Type something in the search box on the home page and hit Search. Results come back as a grid. You can also search inside a specific creator from there page.

---

## Settings

![Settings](/docs/img/11-settings.png)

The settings dialog has:

- **Default Subtitles** - on or off by default
- **Preferred Quality** - pick a default resolution (Auto, 720p, 1080p, etc). The player uses this when you hit play.
- **Subtitle Color** - white, yellow, pink and others
- **Subtitle Position** - normal or higher
- **Auto-play Next** - keep playing the next video automatically
- **Version** - the app version
- **Log out** - signs you out of your Floatplane account

Press **Done** to close.

## Remote buttons

A quick reference for the remote:

| Button       | What it does                                  |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| Up / Down    | Move between rows or menu items               |
| Left / Right | Move within a row, or open/close the sidebar  |
| Enter        | Open or select                                |
| Back         | Go back                                       |
| Home         | Jump to Home                                  |
| Info         | Toggle the technical info panel in the player |
| CC           | Toggle subtitles in the player                |
| 1-9, 0       | Jump to a creator row on Home (1st to 10th)   |
| Red          | Open Settings                                 |

## Troubleshooting

**Video wont play.** If the player shows an error, try pressing Back and opening the video again. On the simulator the CDN sometimes blocks the stream, the real TV handles it better.

**Videos are slow to load thumbnails.** Give it a second, the app loads small thumbnails first and upgrades them.

**Watch Later is empty.** Its stored on the TV, so if you cleared the app data or reinstalled, the list is gone. Sorry.

**Keyboard does not show up for comments.** Yeah, commenting is currently disabled in the app for this reason. Read only for now.
